\section{Advantages}

\textbf{Cost Benefits:} Zero platform fees (only minimal gas costs), immediate payment release upon mutual approval, and transparent on-chain pricing with no hidden charges.

\textbf{Security Benefits:} Trustless operations through smart contracts, immutable blockchain records, secure escrow without intermediaries, and cryptographic transaction signatures.

\textbf{Data Sovereignty:} User-owned IPFS data, censorship resistance, and data portability across platforms.

\textbf{Transparency:} Open-source auditable contracts, verifiable transactions, and fair mutual approval mechanism.

\section{Limitations}

\textbf{Current Limitations:}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item No dispute resolution mechanism (requires mutual agreement)
    \item No automatic deadline enforcement
    \item Only ETH payment support (no ERC-20 tokens)
    \item No on-chain reputation system
    \item Limited to local development testing
    \item Variable gas costs during network congestion
    \item Technical barrier for crypto-newbies
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Mitigation Strategies:} Third-party arbitration, Layer 2 integration for reduced costs, reputation system planned for future releases, and improved UI/UX to lower technical barriers.

\section{Applications}

SkillChain can be applied across various industries: software development (web, mobile, smart contracts), creative services (design, writing, video), professional services (legal, financial, marketing), technical services (data analysis, ML, QA), and educational services (tutoring, course creation).

Emerging use cases include DAO contributions with transparent payment tracking, open source bug bounties, academic research collaboration, and remote team payment coordination. The platform targets crypto-native freelancers, Web3 startups, and remote-first companies seeking lower fees and faster payments.
